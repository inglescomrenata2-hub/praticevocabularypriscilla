1. I'm always open to (hearing) feedback from my supervisor. (feedback is uncountable; "open to" is followed by -ing)

3. Let's stick to the timeline we agreed on with the client.
4. The associate carried out the review very carefully and on time. (carry on = continue)
5. I get along well with my classmates, even though I'm the only Brazilian.
6. During the Q&A, the professor asked me a question about tort law.
